# Aether Launch Strategy

## Positioning

> **Aether is the AI companion for the internet** — a browser built for
> people who think, build and ship.

We are not "Chrome with a sidebar". We are an operating environment for
people who use the web like an IDE — researchers, founders, devs, creators.

Closest neighbours: **Arc**, **Dia**, **SigmaOS**, **Brave**.
Differentiators:

- True multi-agent autonomy.
- Local-first AI with end-to-end encrypted memory.
- A plugin SDK that turns the browser into a programmable surface.

## Phase 0 — Private alpha (this MVP)

- 100 hand-picked alpha testers (Twitter, HN, AI Discords).
- Weekly builds; Discord channel for feedback.
- Goal: prove the AI sidebar + agents deliver "I cannot go back to Chrome".

## Phase 1 — Public beta

- ProductHunt + HackerNews launch.
- Long-form launch post: "Why we built another browser".
- Free tier opens; Pro waitlist.
- 50 k beta testers target.

## Phase 2 — General availability

- Pro paid tier opens at $19/mo (early-bird $9 for first 90 days).
- Plugin marketplace soft-launch with 25 hand-picked plugins.
- Press: TechCrunch, The Verge, Stratechery, Latent Space.

## Phase 3 — Team & Enterprise

- Team tier shipped + 5 design-partner accounts.
- Enterprise sales-led motion.
- SOC 2 Type I certification.

## Content engine

- "AI browser of the week" newsletter (Substack).
- Public roadmap (Linear / Productlane).
- Open-source design system + agent SDK (separate repo, MIT).
- A weekly demo on YouTube — "Aether shipped: …".

## Community

- Discord server with separate channels per agent.
- Bi-weekly "Aether for builders" office hours.
- Bug bounty after Beta.

## Distribution

- macOS DMG (signed + notarised), Windows MSI (signed), Linux AppImage/Deb.
- Auto-update via electron-updater on a delta channel.
- App stores (Mac, Microsoft Store) for visibility, even though they're
  rarely the primary download path for power-user browsers.

## Pricing strategy

See `monetization.md`. Free remains useful; Pro is the volume tier; Team
is the GTM motion for organic growth in companies; Enterprise is sales-led.

## KPIs

- D1 retention > 35 % (alpha), 50 % (beta).
- Weekly active sessions > 5 per Pro user.
- Net promoter score > 60 by GA.
- Pro conversion 1 % year-1, 3 % year-2.
